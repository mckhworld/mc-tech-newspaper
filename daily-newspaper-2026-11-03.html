https://dev.to/codewithrajat/doubleclick-jacking-why-your-clickjacking-headers-never-see-it-coming-1hgh📅 2026-11-04 03:00 UTC+8

DoubleClickjacking is a new class of attack, published by researcher Paulos Yibelo in December 2024, that completely bypasses X-Frame-Options, CSP frame-ancestors, and SameSite cookies because it never places the victim's page inside a frame. Instead, the attacker opens a small window on top asking the user to double-click, while the window underneath silently navigates to a sensitive page such as an OAuth consent screen; the first click closes the top window and the second click lands on the real Approve button. The article explains why traditional server-side headers are useless here and provides an Angular component as a client-side gate to defend against it.

DoubleClickjacking 係一種新嘅攻擊手法，2024年12月由研究員 Paulos Yibelo 發表，佢完全繞過咗 X-Frame-Options、CSP frame-ancestors 同 SameSite cookies，因為佢根本唔會將你嘅網頁放入 frame 入面。攻擊者喺上面開一個細窗口叫你雙擊，同時下面個窗口已經靜悄悄跳去你嘅 OAuth 授權頁面，第一下點擊收咗上面個窗口，第二下就撳落咗真正嘅 Approve 按鈕。篇文章講清楚點解傳統嘅 server-side headers 完全冇用，仲畀咗一個 Angular 組件做 client-side 嘅防護。
